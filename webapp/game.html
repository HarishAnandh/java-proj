Disaster Safety Knowledge Hub

Standardized operating procedures to protect yourself and your family.

🌍

Earthquake Protocol

Drop, Cover, and Hold On! Take cover under sturdy tables. Stay away from glass windows and power lines. Never use elevators during or right after a tremor.

🌊

Flash Flood Safety

Turn Around, Don't Drown! Never walk or drive through moving water. Just 6 inches can sweep you off your feet. Move immediately to higher elevation.

🔥

Structural Fire & Smoke

Crawl Low Under Smoke! Clean, breathable air stays near the ground. Cover your nose with a damp cloth and use stairwells to evacuate.

🌀

Cyclone & High Winds

Shelter in Interior Rooms! Stay away from exterior windows and doors. Disconnect electrical appliances and secure loose rooftop objects.

☢️

Gas Leak / Hazmat

Evacuate Upwind! If you detect a rotten-egg sulfur odor, evacuate immediately. Do not switch lights on or use phones inside the structure.

🌊

Tsunami Shore Warnings

Rapid Coastline Receding = Move Up! When coastal water draws back suddenly, a massive wave is approaching. Evacuate inland and at least 30m uphill.
